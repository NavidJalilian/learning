Boss: the curveball barrage

Step 3 · "what happens when…?"

Now the interviewer stops nodding and starts poking holes. Nine curveballs, mixed from every quest in the chapter. Each right answer costs 1.5 minutes; a wrong one costs a heart and an extra minute of backtracking.

Honest caveats from the curveballs. (1) W + R > N only guarantees you read the latest write when the W writers and R readers are real replicas. With a sloppy quorum, stand-in nodes can count toward W, so the overlap isn't guaranteed — DDIA calls it a durability promise, not a true quorum[6]. (2) Hints don't live forever: Cassandra keeps them for 3 hours by default; after that, only anti-entropy repair (the Merkle-tree comparison) catches the replica up[5].
