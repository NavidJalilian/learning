## Why
Navid wants to walk into a system design interview round and hold a senior-level
conversation: take a vague prompt ("design a key-value store"), scope it, and
reason out loud in trade-offs. The book is Alex Xu's _System Design Interview_
(Vol. 1), worked through chapter by chapter. This is the interview-shaped twin of
the senior-developer track's "hold my own in a staff system-design conversation."

## Success looks like
- Given a book-style prompt, can state requirements and scope in the first 5 minutes.
- Can name the trade-off behind every major choice ("CP buys correctness at the
  cost of refusing requests during a network split").
- Can sketch and explain each chapter's final design from memory, without the book.
- Can answer the interviewer's "what happens when X fails?" follow-up.

## Constraints
- Short lessons alongside a full-time job.
- **Gamified lessons**: quests, XP, interactive simulations — not walls of text.
- Source of truth is the book; lessons follow its chapter order unless asked otherwise.

## Out of scope (for now)
- DSA / coding rounds (see the dsa-interviews track).
- Leadership & soft-skill topics (see the senior-developer track).
